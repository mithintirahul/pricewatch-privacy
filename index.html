PriceWatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

What PriceWatch does

PriceWatch helps you monitor product prices you select and compare exact-product offers where a retailer exposes usable data. This policy covers the PriceWatch Chrome extension. The website hosting this policy may have its own privacy policy.

Information used for price watching

When you start the picker on a page, PriceWatch reads the selected page’s URL, title, chosen price, currency and relevant product details. Depending on the retailer, those details can include a barcode or product identifier, selected configuration, condition or grade, seller, stock status and membership-price information. It also records the page location of the price so it can check it again.

When you save a watch, PriceWatch stores the watch, optional alert target, observed price history, check times, comparison results, notification-delivery state and queued checks in your Chrome profile using local extension storage. A pending price pick is also stored locally until saved or cancelled. PriceWatch does not use Chrome storage sync for these records.

PriceWatch does not monitor your general browsing history or unrelated pages. It does not request your name, email address, payment-card details, account passwords, financial-account data, precise location or personal communications. Avoid selecting pages containing information you do not want saved as part of a product title or URL.

Retailer requests and data sharing

To perform checks, PriceWatch opens the HTTPS product pages you selected. To compare prices, it opens HTTPS retailer searches using the product title or product identifier and visits candidate product pages. Your browser sends those requests directly to the retailers. Retailers may receive normal web-request information such as your IP address, browser information and existing retailer cookies, and their own privacy policies apply.

PriceWatch does not operate a price-collection server, advertising network, analytics service or data-selling service. It does not upload your watch list or price history to a PriceWatch server. This build does not send product data to AI services. The extension does not add its own affiliate tags to comparison URLs.

Notifications and scheduled checks

PriceWatch can show local Chrome notifications containing product names and prices. Checks run through Chrome’s background extension facilities while Chrome is running. Removing a watch stops future scheduled checks for it; an already-open check may finish without restoring the removed watch.

Retention and deletion

Saved watches and their histories remain in your Chrome profile until you remove them or delete the extension’s stored data. Price observations older than the extension’s retention window are pruned; the current build retains observations for up to approximately 35 days for its observed-low feature. Removing the extension clears its local extension storage. Deleting PriceWatch data does not delete retailer account information or normal Chrome browsing records, which are controlled separately by those services and Chrome.

PriceWatch does not maintain a server-side backup. Moving to a different Chrome profile, uninstalling, or loading an unpacked copy from a different folder may make an existing watch list unavailable.

Permissions and control

The activeTab and scripting permissions support the picker and page-price reading. The storage permission saves watches locally. Alarms schedule checks, and notifications display alerts. Optional HTTPS site access is requested for the sites needed by watches and enabled automatic comparisons. You can decline or revoke site access in Chrome; affected checks will not work until access is available.

Limited Use

PriceWatch uses page and product data only to provide the price-watching and comparison features you choose. Its use and transfer of data comply with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. PriceWatch does not sell user data, use it for personalized advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility. The publisher does not receive your watch list through the extension and does not permit human review of extension data except when you explicitly choose to provide specific information for support or when required for security or legal obligations.

Contact and changes

For privacy or support questions, use the publisher contact information on PriceWatch’s Chrome Web Store listing. Updates to this policy will be published here with a revised date. Material changes to extension data practices will also require corresponding in-product and store disclosures.
